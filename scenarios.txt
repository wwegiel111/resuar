1) Run cool water over the area of the burn or soak it in a cool water bath (not ice water). Keep the area under water for at least 5 to 30 minutes. A clean, cold, wet towel will help reduce pain.
2) Calm and reassure the person.
3) After flushing or soaking the burn, cover it with a dry, sterile bandage or clean dressing.
4) Protect the burn from pressure and friction.
5) Over-the-counter ibuprofen or acetaminophen can help relieve pain and swelling. 6) Do not give aspirin to children under 12.
7) Once the skin has cooled, moisturizing lotion containing aloe and an antibiotic also can help.


FOR MINOR CUTS

1) Wash your hands with soap or antibacterial cleanser to prevent infection.
2) Then, wash the cut thoroughly with mild soap and water.
3) Use direct pressure to stop the bleeding.
4) Apply antibacterial ointment and a clean bandage that will not stick to the wound.
